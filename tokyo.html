Taste of Tokyo

A Japanese dish made with seasoned rice and ingredients such as seafood and vegetables.

Ramen

A comforting noodle soup served with broth, toppings, and flavorful ingredients.

Tempura

Seafood or vegetables lightly battered and deep-fried until crispy.

Matcha

A traditional Japanese green tea known for its distinctive flavor and vibrant color.

One Day in Tokyo

A suggested route to explore Tokyo in one day.

09:00 AM

Meiji Shrine

Start your day with a peaceful walk through the forested grounds of this famous shrine.

11:30 AM

Yanaka Ginza

Explore this nostalgic shopping street, browse local shops, and try traditional Japanese snacks.

02:00 PM

Asakusa and Senso-ji

Discover traditional Tokyo around the temple and Nakamise shopping street.

06:00 PM

Shimokitazawa

End your day among vintage shops, cozy cafés, and the neighborhood’s creative atmosphere.
