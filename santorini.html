Travel Tips

Wear comfortable shoes for walking on Santorini’s steep streets and stairs, Bring sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat during sunny days, Visit popular viewpoints early or near sunset to avoid the biggest crowds.

Top Attractions

Discover the most beautiful places in Santorini.

Oia Village

A picturesque village known for its white houses, blue domes, and beautiful sunsets.

Fira Town

Santorini’s lively capital, with caldera views, shops, cafés, and restaurants.

Santorini Caldera

A dramatic volcanic landscape with sweeping views across the Aegean Sea.

Taste of Santorini

Discover the delicious flavors of Santorini.

Fava

A creamy split-pea purée, often served with olive oil and onions.

Fresh Seafood

Fish and seafood commonly served at seaside tavernas.

Tomatokeftedes

Crispy tomato fritters made with Santorini’s local tomatoes.

Greek Frappé

A refreshing iced coffee made with instant coffee, water, and milk or sugar if desired.

One Day in Santorini

A suggested route to explore Santorini in one day.

09:00 AM

Fira

Start with a walk through Fira’s quiet streets and enjoy breakfast with a caldera view.

11:00 AM

Three Bells of Fira

Visit the famous blue-domed church and take photos overlooking the sea.

03:00 PM

Megalochori

Have lunch in the village square and see its traditional houses and bell towers.

07:00 PM

Pyrgos Village

Explore the traditional lanes and climb toward the old castle for views across the island.
