Top Attractions

Explore colorful mosaics, creative architecture, and beautiful views of Barcelona.

Casa Batlló

Gothic Quarter

Walk through narrow medieval streets, charming squares, and historic buildings.

Taste of Barcelona

Discover the delicious flavors of Barcelona.

Tapas

Small Spanish dishes with different flavors, perfect for sharing.

Pa amb Tomàquet

Traditional Catalan bread topped with fresh tomato and olive oil.

Churros

Fried dough pastries commonly served with thick hot chocolate.

Horchata de Chufa

A traditional Spanish drink made from tiger nuts, water, and sugar. It is usually served cold.
