Taste of Paris

Discover the delicious flavors of Paris.

Croissant

A buttery, flaky pastry commonly enjoyed for breakfast.

Ratatouille

A traditional French vegetable dish made with tomatoes, zucchini, eggplant, and herbs.

Macarons

Colorful and delicate almond-based cookies with sweet fillings.

Coq au Vin

A traditional French dish of chicken slowly cooked with red wine, mushrooms, and herbs.

One Day in Paris

A suggested route to explore Paris in one day.

09:00 AM

Eiffel Tower

Start your day by visiting the iconic Eiffel Tower and enjoying the beautiful view.

11:00 AM

Seine River Cruise

Enjoy a relaxing boat ride and admire Paris from the river.

03:00 PM

Arc de Triomphe

End your day with a walk along the Champs-Élysées and visit the Arc de Triomphe

07:00 PM

Montmartre & Sacré-Cœur

Explore the charming streets of Montmartre, enjoy the lively atmosphere, and admire the sparkling lights of Paris from the Sacré-Cœur viewpoint.
