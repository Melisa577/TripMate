Travel Tips

Dress modestly when visiting temples, carry cash for small purchases, use a reputable transport service, and bring sunscreen and insect repellent.

Top Attractions

Discover the most beautiful places in Bali.

Uluwatu Temple

Visit this clifftop temple overlooking the Indian Ocean and enjoy its dramatic coastal views.

Tegallalang Rice Terraces

Explore Bali’s famous green rice terraces and admire the traditional farming landscape.

Ubud Monkey Forest

Walk through a lush forest sanctuary with ancient temples and free-roaming monkeys.

Taste of Bali

Discover the delicious flavors of bali.

Nasi Goreng

Indonesian fried rice cooked with seasonings, vegetables, and topped with an egg.

Satay

Grilled chicken, commonly served with a rich peanut sauce.

Gado-Gado

A refreshing mix of vegetables, tofu, and eggs topped with peanut sauce.

Es Cendol

A sweet iced drink made with coconut milk, palm sugar, and green rice-flour jelly.

One Day in Bali

A suggested route to explore Bali in one day.

09:00 AM

Tegallalang Rice Terraces

Start the day with a walk among Bali’s famous green rice terraces.

11:00 AM

Tirta Empul Temple

Visit this sacred water temple and learn about Balinese Hindu traditions.

03:00 PM

Ubud Palace

Explore the historic palace area and enjoy lunch at a nearby local café.

07:00 PM

Goa Gajah

Explore an ancient sanctuary known for its carved cave entrance and historic bathing pools.
