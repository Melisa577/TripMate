Know Before You Go

Language

Thai

Currency

Thai Baht (THB)

Climate

Tropical

Best Time to Visit

Late Winter and Spring

Travel Tips

Wear comfortable clothes because Bangkok can be hot and humid, Use the BTS Skytrain and MRT subway to avoid traffic, Dress respectfully when visiting temples and religious sites.

Top Attractions

Discover the most beautiful places in bangkok.

Grand Palace

Explore Thailand’s magnificent royal complex with its beautiful architecture and rich history.

Wat Pho

Discover one of Bangkok’s oldest temples, home to the famous Reclining Buddha.

Jim Thompson House

Discover traditional Thai architecture and the fascinating history of Thai silk.

Taste of Bangkok

Discover the delicious flavors of Bangkok.

Pad Thai

A famous Thai stir-fried noodle dish with shrimps, tofu, peanuts, and vegetables.

Mango Sticky Rice

A popular Thai dessert made with sweet mango, sticky rice, and coconut milk.

Green Curry

A traditional Thai curry made with coconut milk, green chili paste, and fresh herbs.

Thai Iced Tea

A traditional Thai drink made with strong black tea, milk, and sugar, usually served over ice.
